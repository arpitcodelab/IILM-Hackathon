## High-Level Product Architecture

MediBridge AI unifies:
1. **Zero-Barrier Emergency Guidance**: Accessible without login or verification, emergency map tracking, and hospital command triage.
2. **Identity & Record Protection**: Tiered verification (India ABHA/Aadhaar and International Passport) required before protected medical record access.
3. **Clinical Intelligence & Source Provenance**: Document OCR and clinical summarization with page citations, uncertainty flags, and patient correction dialogs.
4. **Provider Discovery & Cost Transparency**: Verified hospital directory with detailed fee breakdowns (doctor fee, hospital charges, consumable estimates) and no fake slots.
5. **Government Healthcare Schemes Recommendation**: Preliminary evaluation for PM-JAY and state health schemes with document checklists and clear liability calculations.
6. **Doctor Consultation Workspace**: Daily appointments queue, teleconsultation room, voice-to-text prescription authoring with digital signature authentication, and Jan Aushadhi generic medicine comparison.
7. **Hospital Emergency Command Panel**: Real-time multi-column triage, live ambulance GPS tracking, hospital capacity gauges, and instant ER prep coordination.

# Clinical Chemistry, Hematology, and Metabolic Laboratory Reference Ranges

SOURCE METADATA:
Title: Standard Clinical Diagnostic Laboratory Reference Ranges and Clinical Chemistry Interpretation
Publisher: Consensus Clinical Pathology & Laboratory Medicine Guidelines
Publication Date: 2024
URL: https://www.aacc.org/clinical-guidelines

COMPLETE BLOOD COUNT (CBC) REFERENCE RANGES (ADULT):
- Hemoglobin (Hb):
  * Adult Males: 13.5 - 17.5 g/dL (135 - 175 g/L)
  * Adult Females: 12.0 - 15.5 g/dL (120 - 155 g/L)
  * Clinical Flag: Values < 10.0 g/dL indicate moderate anemia; < 7.0 g/dL indicate severe anemia requiring urgent evaluation.
- Hematocrit (Hct):
  * Adult Males: 38.8% - 50.0%
  * Adult Females: 34.9% - 44.5%
- White Blood Cell (WBC) Count: 4.5 - 11.0 x 10^3/uL (4.5 - 11.0 x 10^9/L)
  * Leukocytosis (> 11.0): Common causes include infection, systemic inflammation, acute physical stress, corticosteroid therapy.
  * Leukopenia (< 4.0): Common causes include viral infections, autoimmune conditions, bone marrow suppression, medications.
- Platelet Count: 150 - 450 x 10^3/uL
  * Thrombocytopenia (< 150): Increased bleeding tendency if < 50; spontaneous bleeding risk if < 20.
  * Thrombocytosis (> 450): Reactive thrombocytosis vs. primary myeloproliferative disorders.

BASIC METABOLIC PANEL & RENAL FUNCTION:
- Serum Sodium: 136 - 145 mEq/L (mmol/L)
  * Hyponatremia (< 135): Syndrome of Inappropriate Antidiuretic Hormone (SIADH), heart failure, thiazide diuretic use.
  * Hypernatremia (> 145): Dehydration, osmotic diuresis, diabetes insipidus.
- Serum Potassium: 3.5 - 5.0 mEq/L (mmol/L)
  * Hypokalemia (< 3.5): Loop/thiazide diuretics, GI losses. Risk of cardiac arrhythmias, muscle weakness.
  * Hyperkalemia (> 5.0): Acute/chronic renal failure, ACE inhibitors, ARBs, potassium-sparing diuretics. Critical value > 6.0 mEq/L requiring emergency ECG and intervention.
- Serum Chloride: 98 - 106 mEq/L (mmol/L)
- Serum Bicarbonate / Total CO2: 22 - 29 mEq/L (mmol/L)
- Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN): 7 - 20 mg/dL (2.5 - 7.1 mmol/L)
- Serum Creatinine:
  * Adult Males: 0.7 - 1.3 mg/dL (62 - 115 umol/L)
  * Adult Females: 0.5 - 1.1 mg/dL (44 - 97 umol/L)
- Estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR, CKD-EPI equation):
  * Normal: > 90 mL/min/1.73m2
  * Mild reduction (Stage 2 CKD): 60 - 89 mL/min/1.73m2
  * Moderate reduction (Stage 3a/3b CKD): 30 - 59 mL/min/1.73m2 (medication dose adjustments often required)
  * Severe reduction (Stage 4 CKD): 15 - 29 mL/min/1.73m2
  * Kidney failure (Stage 5 CKD): < 15 mL/min/1.73m2

LIPID PANEL REFERENCE VALUES (FASTING ADULT):
- Total Cholesterol: Desirable < 200 mg/dL (5.18 mmol/L); Borderline high 200 - 239 mg/dL; High >= 240 mg/dL.
- Low-Density Lipoprotein (LDL) Cholesterol:
  * Optimal: < 100 mg/dL (2.59 mmol/L)
  * In patients with established ASCVD or diabetes: Target < 70 mg/dL (1.81 mmol/L) or < 55 mg/dL for very high risk.
  * Borderline high: 130 - 159 mg/dL; High: 160 - 189 mg/dL; Very high: >= 190 mg/dL.
- High-Density Lipoprotein (HDL) Cholesterol:
  * Men: Desirable > 40 mg/dL (1.04 mmol/L)
  * Women: Desirable > 50 mg/dL (1.30 mmol/L)
  * Low HDL (< 40 in men, < 50 in women) is an independent cardiovascular risk factor.
- Fasting Serum Triglycerides:
  * Normal: < 150 mg/dL (1.70 mmol/L)
  * Borderline high: 150 - 199 mg/dL
  * High: 200 - 499 mg/dL
  * Very high: >= 500 mg/dL (significant risk of acute pancreatitis).

LIVER FUNCTION & ENZYME TESTS:
- Alanine Aminotransferase (ALT): 7 - 56 U/L (specific for hepatocellular injury)
- Aspartate Aminotransferase (AST): 10 - 40 U/L
- Alkaline Phosphatase (ALP): 44 - 147 U/L (marker of biliary obstruction, cholestasis, or bone turnover)
- Total Bilirubin: 0.1 - 1.2 mg/dL (1.7 - 20.5 umol/L)
- Serum Albumin: 3.5 - 5.0 g/dL (35 - 50 g/L)

THYROID FUNCTION TESTS:
- Thyroid Stimulating Hormone (TSH): 0.40 - 4.00 mIU/L (uIU/mL)
  * Primary Hypothyroidism: Elevated TSH (> 4.5) with low or normal Free T4.
  * Primary Hyperthyroidism: Suppressed TSH (< 0.1) with elevated Free T4 / Free T3.
- Free Thyroxine (Free T4): 0.8 - 1.8 ng/dL (10 - 23 pmol/L)
